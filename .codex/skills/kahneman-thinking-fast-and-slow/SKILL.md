## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

### System 1 / System 2
Use **System 1** to name fast, automatic, associative, effortless, emotional, and story-building processes. Use **System 2** to name slow, controlled, effortful, rule-following, and self-monitoring processes. The practical move is not to suppress System 1; it is to know when its output is only a hypothesis and when System 2 must check base rates, logic, sample size, incentives, and alternatives.

### WYSIATI: What You See Is All There Is
System 1 builds the most coherent story possible from available evidence and often ignores missing evidence. Use WYSIATI when a decision feels obvious from a thin case file, a vivid anecdote, or a polished narrative. Ask: what would I need to know before this story deserves confidence?

### Heuristic Substitution
When a target question is hard, System 1 answers an easier related question: "Do I like this person?" instead of "Is this person competent?"; "Can I recall examples?" instead of "How frequent is this?"; "Does this story fit?" instead of "What is the probability?" Use this model to debug fast judgments.

### Base Rates, Regression, and the Outside View
For predictions, start with the reference class. Then adjust cautiously for diagnostic evidence and regress toward the mean when evidence is noisy. This is Kahneman's recurring correction to intuitive forecasting, planning, hiring, investing, and expert judgment.

### Prospect Theory
Choices are evaluated as gains and losses around a reference point, not only by final wealth. The value function is steeper for losses than gains, and decision weights distort probability. Use prospect theory for pricing, negotiation, risk policy, insurance, product defaults, and communication about uncertainty.

### Remembering Self / Experiencing Self
The experiencing self lives through moments; the remembering self stores stories, peaks, endings, and evaluations. Many choices are made by the remembering self even when they affect the experiencing self. Use this distinction in service design, health, travel, work, and happiness measurement.

### Decision Hygiene
Kahneman's practical advice is procedural: independent judgments before discussion, algorithms where valid, external views for plans, premortems for optimism, broad framing for repeated risks, and explicit reference classes for forecasts.
